# Brand Core — Rohit Builds

Single source of truth for who the site is, what it says, and how it presents itself. Agents building or editing this site must read this file first.

## Name

**Rohit Builds**

The personal brand name. Never shorten it to "RB" and never revert to any earlier branding ("Education Reimagined", "RBPN"). Visual treatment is a name-only wordmark in the brand gradient (no logo mark).

## Positioning

Personal brand portfolio. The home page is the careers presentation: "Designing data platforms that are readable, reliable, and ready to scale" — an Azure Data Engineer evolving into a platform-minded architect. Every passion project lives on its own page beneath.

## What the site is

A portfolio of passion projects by Rohit Bhandari, with the personal brand front and center.

- **Home (portfolio / careers)** — the identity card: hero, core strengths, case studies, experience, credentials, contact. Built like a hiring page, not a resume dump.
- **Flagship projects** — News (curated AI news portal, editorial voice) and Shop (storefront project) are the two featured passion projects.
- **Supporting projects** — the education system (5 age groups: 4-7 Bot Buddies, 8-11 Code Kids, 12-15 Code Quest, 15-17 AI Lab, 18+ Career Launcher), courses, and learning tools (Pomoflow focus timer, Flashcard, Interview prep, Jobtool).

## Origin story

Started as Rohit's own study tools — Pomoflow and flashcard apps he built for himself — which grew into a full education system and a public portfolio of shipping projects.

## Tagline / mission

Site title: **Rohit Builds — The new education system.** (Homepage og/meta uses "Rohit Builds — Azure Data Engineer & Builder".)

Homepage hero (personal): *Designing data platforms that are readable, reliable, and ready to scale.*

Education mission: *Make world-class education accessible, personalized, and engaging for every human on the planet. No gatekeepers. No one-size-fits-all. Just learning that works.*

## Audience

Hiring managers and recruiters (the home/portfolio page), the AI-curious public (News), and learners of every age (education system and tools).

## Value proposition

- The home page proves engineering credibility with verified credentials and case studies.
- The projects show a builder at work: real, hands-on, shipping features.
- News and Shop lead the project lineup; everything else supports the brand.

## About (fillable bio)

Rohit Bhandari — an Azure Data Engineer who builds learning tools and AI education products in his spare time, currently growing toward platform architecture.

## Personality (short form)

Maker, educator, and builder. Confident without hyping; technical without text-speak. Optimistic about AI's potential to fix education. Dry and factual across tool pages. Full voice guidance in `brand/voice.md`.

## Visual identity (short form)

Brand gradient `135deg #667eea → #764ba2` (indigo to violet). Primary fonts: Manrope (UI), Inter (body), Playfair Display (editorial headings), JetBrains Mono (code). Light theme only, no dark mode. Full tokens in `brand/visual.md`. Single source of truth for styles: `public/assets/css/brand.css`.
