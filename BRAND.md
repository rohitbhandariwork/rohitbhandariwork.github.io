# Brand Core — Rohit Builds

Single source of truth for who the site is, what it says, and how it presents itself. Agents building or editing this site must read this file first.

## Name

**Rohit Builds**

The personal brand name. Never shorten it to "RB" and never revert to any earlier branding ("Education Reimagined", "RBPN").

## One-line positioning

A portfolio of passion projects built by Rohit Bhandari — an AI-powered education system for every age, a curated AI news portal, and learning tools — presented like a maker's workbench, not a resume dump.

## What the site is

A portfolio. Different passion projects, each on its own page:

- **Education system** for all ages — 5 age groups: 4-7 (Bot Buddies), 8-11 (Code Kids), 12-15 (Code Quest), 15-17 (AI Lab), 18+ (Career Launcher).
- **Learning tools** — Pomoflow (focus timer), Flashcard, Interview prep, Jobtool.
- **AI news portal** — a daily-ish digest of AI developments.
- **Shop** — a storefront project.
- **Careers** — `/portfolio/` presenting the author as an Azure Data Engineer evolving into a platform-minded architect.

## Tagline / mission

Site title: **Rohit Builds — The new education system.**

Homepage hero: *The new education system — AI-powered, personalized, and free for every age group.*

Mission: *Make world-class education accessible, personalized, and engaging for every human on the planet. No gatekeepers. No one-size-fits-all. Just learning that works.*

Footer style (older copy, keep or update deliberately): *Built for learners, by learners.*

## Audience

Learners of every age, hiring managers reviewing `/portfolio/`, and the general AI-curious public reading the news portal.

## Value proposition

- Everything is free and open, aimed at world-class education for everyone.
- Projects are real, hands-on, and shipping — not mocked-up.
- Each project is self-contained and explorable on its own page.

## Personality (short form)

Maker, educator, and builder. Confident without hyping; technical without text-speak. Optimistic about AI's potential to fix education. Full voice guidance in `brand/voice.md`.

## Visual identity (short form)

Brand gradient `135deg #667eea → #764ba2` (indigo to violet). Primary fonts: Manrope (UI), Inter (body), Playfair Display (editorial headings), JetBrains Mono (code). Full tokens in `brand/visual.md`. Single source of truth for styles: `public/assets/css/brand.css`.
