How to Use Pomoflow

🎯 The Core Idea

Your first session is just 5 minutes — small enough to start, honest enough to finish. Every completed focus adds 5 minutes to your next one, so your concentration grows session by session, the same way you grow a skill.

🔁 The Flow
5 min → 10 min → 15 min → 20 min … your focus limit, pushed a little further each time.
  • Work — hit Start and go deep until the ring closes.
  • Break — the timer switches to a break; hit End Break when you're ready.
  • Interrupt — struggling? Reset back to 5 min anytime. No shame, it's still +10 XP.
🎮 The Game
  • Every finished session earns +10 XP and keeps your day streak alive.
  • Level up through ranks: Flow Starter, Concentration Pro, Deep Worker, Focus Master… up to Unshakeable.
  • The 7 dots above your progress bar are your week — don't let one go grey.
🎵 Sound & Effects

Pick between Silent, Tick, or Soothing (soft rain) while you focus. Toggle the effects pill on for completion confetti and floating XP. Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.
