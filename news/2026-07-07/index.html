Biggest Tech Stories of the Week: July 7, 2026

This week saw significant developments in the tech industry, from supply deals to layoffs and AI research. We'll break down the top four stories that made headlines.

Broadcom Extends Apple Chip Supply Deal Through 2031 
**Key Takeaway:** Broadcom has secured a five-year extension of its contract to supply custom ASIC chips to Apple, demonstrating the tech giant's commitment to custom chip design and partnership with Broadcom.
**Why It Matters:** This deal highlights the importance of semiconductors in the tech industry and showcases Broadcom's long-term growth prospects.

As part of this extension, Broadcom will continue to supply Apple with custom chips, including those used in iPhones and Macs. While revenue figures for this contract were not disclosed, it's worth noting that Broadcom's market capitalization is around $140 billion.

Microsoft Cuts 4,800 Jobs Across Xbox Unit 
**Key Takeaway:** Microsoft has announced the elimination of approximately 4,800 jobs across its Xbox unit, highlighting the challenges faced by gaming companies in today's competitive market.
**Why It Matters:** This move may signal a shift away from its gaming business, but others see it as a necessary step to adapt to changing market conditions and prioritize profitability.

The Xbox unit is one of Microsoft's most significant revenue streams, so this decision will likely have significant implications for the company's overall financials. With Microsoft's market capitalization sitting at around $2 trillion, this move may have far-reaching consequences.

Google Launches 2 New AI Models 
**Key Takeaway:** Google has announced the launch of two new AI models, highlighting its continued investment in AI research and development.
**Why It Matters:** This development could have significant implications for Google's business model and revenue growth, as AI becomes increasingly integral to the company's operations.

Google's focus on AI has been a major driver of innovation in recent years, and this latest move is no exception. With Alphabet's market capitalization sitting at around $1.3 trillion, this announcement could have significant short-term impacts on investor sentiment.

Meta Orders Removal of Child Abuse Content from Instagram 
**Key Takeaway:** India has ordered Meta to remove child abuse content from Instagram, highlighting concerns about online safety and child protection.
**Why It Matters:** This move demonstrates the importance of social media companies taking proactive steps to prioritize user safety, particularly when it comes to vulnerable populations like children.

With approximately 1.2 billion active users on Instagram (as of 2022), this decision will likely be closely watched by regulators and users alike. Meta's response to this issue could have significant implications for the company's reputation and future growth prospects.
