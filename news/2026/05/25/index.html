Microsoft's Acquisition of Activision Blizzard Officially Closes

After a long regulatory battle, Microsoft has officially acquired Activision Blizzard for a staggering $68.7 billion. This deal, first announced in January 2022, was greenlit by regulatory authorities on May 24, 2026, marking a major milestone in the gaming industry.

The Details

  • Activision Blizzard reported revenues of $8.39 billion in 2023.
  • The company boasts over 400 million monthly active users across its game titles.

Why It Matters

This acquisition significantly consolidates Microsoft's position in the gaming market, particularly with blockbuster franchises like Call of Duty and World of Warcraft now under its umbrella. As subscription gaming continues to grow, Microsoft is poised to compete more aggressively. However, critics argue that this could lead to a monopoly in gaming services, potentially diminishing competition and impacting player choices. There are renewed calls for stricter regulations on tech mergers.
