Google's AI Developments and Bard Updates

On October 23, 2023, Google made headlines with major updates to its AI language model, Bard, which is now integrated into various Google products, including Search and Workspace. This significant enhancement aims to bolster user experience and engagement.

The Details

  • Bard saw a 30% increase in engagement following the last update.
  • Google Cloud revenues for Q3 2023 hit $8.5 billion, marking a 30% year-on-year rise.
  • Estimates suggest Bard could contribute an additional $10 billion in annual revenue by 2025.

Why It Matters

In the competitive landscape of generative AI, these advancements signal Google's ambition to remain a frontrunner. Meta's Advertising Revenue Surge

On October 24, 2023, Meta Platforms Inc. announced impressive Q3 earnings showcasing a robust rebound in advertising revenue. This growth highlights the company's adept response to shifting market conditions.

The Details

  • Q3 2023 revenue reached $34 billion, up 24% from the previous year.
  • Monthly active users (MAUs) grew to 3.1 billion globally.
  • Average revenue per user (ARPU) rose to $11.00, up from $9.80 in the last quarter.

Why It Matters

This revenue growth indicates that Meta's ad-targeting algorithms are proving effective despite ongoing regulatory scrutiny over data privacy. However, should brands feel comfortable advertising with Meta, given potential issues regarding misinformation? The rising revenue might suggest that advertisers are choosing short-term gains over ensuring long-term brand safety.
